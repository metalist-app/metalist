Fathom's accent and text colours (fathom.css: blue, cyan, green, yellow,
purple, red, text and comment grey) come from Oceanic Next, by Dmitri
Voronianski, by way of Sublime Text's Mariana, which nudged some of them by
one step (#5FB3B3 -> #5fb4b4, #C594C5 -> #c695c6, #EC5f67 -> #ec5f66,
#FAC863 -> #fac761, #A7ADBA -> #a6acb9). The orange and the background
greys are not Oceanic Next's.

Oceanic Next: https://github.com/voronianski/oceanic-next-color-scheme
